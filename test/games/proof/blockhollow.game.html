Blockhollow: you are a small explorer in a world made of blocks, and you must dig out the 12 hidden crystals while you build whatever you like. Verb: dig a block (A) and place it back (B). Every block you take is one you can build with. Loop: walk around, spot a sparkle on the ground, dig down to the crystal, and build bridges and stairs to get around. Find all 12 to win, then keep building. Curve: there's no danger. Crystals lie 3 to 6 blocks deep, and once the nearby ones are found the rest are farther away. Controls: up/down walk, left/right turn, A dig, B build (with the last kind of block you dug). Walking into a step climbs it. Look: sky blue, grass green, earth brown, stone grey, crystal cyan. Sounds: a crunch, a thunk, a chime.
