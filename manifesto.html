Jean

An AI agent that runs in a browser tab.

Nothing to install. No account. Your files never leave your machine.

Why

AI that does real work has arrived, and it has arrived behind subscriptions, installers and other people’s servers. The best personal agents today ask you to type commands into a terminal, keep a computer running, or hand your files to a company’s cloud. Most people can do none of that, and none of them should have to.

The browser is already on every device. It is the most battle-tested sandbox ever built, it speaks every language, and it belongs to no one. That is where Jean lives.

What Jean does

You give Jean a task and your files. Jean reads them, works things out — running real calculations instead of guessing — and hands back something you can use: a summary, a table, a document. While it works, it shows you what it is doing, and it asks before anything that matters.

What makes Jean different

  • Private by design. Your files stay in your browser. You choose where the thinking happens: on your own device, or with a provider you pick.
  • Free and open. Open source. No subscription. Bring any AI model, or none: on a capable device, Jean can think entirely on your machine.
  • Simple first, deep later. Jean starts as a single box. Everything else — files, calculations, memory, projects, voice — shows up when you need it, like a game that teaches itself.
  • Yours to keep. Everything Jean knows is stored as plain files you can read, back up and take elsewhere.
  • For everyone. Every language and every region, from the first screen to the last answer.
  • Small on purpose. Jean’s code is short enough to read in an afternoon. That is a promise, and a feature.

What Jean won’t do

Jean does not work while you are away: that would need a server watching over your things. When you come back, it tells you what changed since last time.

Jean does not take over your computer. It lives inside the browser’s walls, and it stays there.
