# Flash cards

Cards reviewed over days on Leitner's system: five boxes, reviewed every 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 days. A card they know moves up a box; a card they miss goes back to box 1.

## The file

One `.csv` file per subject, written with `write` and named after the subject in their language. Two lines that Anki reads, then a card per line:

```
#separator:comma
#columns:front,back,box,due
What is 7 × 8?,56,1,2026-10-18
"Capital of Peru",Lima,3,2026-10-21
```

- **front:** the question, the word, or the date to place; **back:** the answer, short. A field with a comma or quotes goes in double quotes.
- **box:** 1 to 5. **due:** the day it is next reviewed, YYYY-MM-DD, counted from today's date.
- Anki imports the file as it is. For Quizlet, which takes a term and its definition, offer a copy with the front and back alone.

## Steps

1. **Make cards from what they missed and what they explained,** not the whole book: one fact per card, the answer in a few words, in their words where they have them. For a language, make each word both ways. Show the cards and let them change any before saving.
2. **New cards** go in box 1, due tomorrow.
3. **A review** takes the cards due today or earlier, at most twenty, lowest box first. Start with one they know well.
4. **Ask one card at a time** with `ask_user`: the front as the question, their answer in their own words, then the back, and how it went: "Knew it", "With a hint", "Not yet".
   - Knew it: up a box, to box 5 at most.
   - With a hint: the same box.
   - Not yet: back to box 1.
   - Then due in as many days as its box gives: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16.
   - Judge their own words fairly: a different wording or a small spelling mistake is still right.
5. **A card overdue is simply due:** review it once, never once per day missed, and never pile the missed days onto today. With more than twenty due, the others wait for the next session.
6. **Save the file again** with the new boxes and dates, with `edit` or `write`.
7. **Say what moved,** not a score: "four cards moved up, and two are solid now" (box 5). No percentages and no streaks. End the review on a card they know.
8. **Offer the next review** as a routine with `schedule`, on the days they study, when they want one.
