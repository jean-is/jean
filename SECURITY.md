# Security

To report a vulnerability, write to [arnaud@jean.is](mailto:arnaud@jean.is) with what you found and how to reproduce it, and please keep it between us until it is fixed. You'll get an answer within three working days, and your name in the fix if you'd like.

In scope: everything in this repository, and jean.is, gateway.jean.is and hst.ing, tested gently. Out of scope: denial of service, social engineering, third-party services Jean connects to, and what people publish at names of hst.ing, which its page's form reports.

[docs/trust/](docs/trust/README.md) holds the rest: the threat model, the penetration test's brief, how releases are signed and how to check one.
